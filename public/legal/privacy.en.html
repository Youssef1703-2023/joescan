Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 · Applies to the JoeScan platform (joescan.me and all subdomains)

A privacy tool should protect your privacy first. This page explains exactly what happens to your data when you use JoeScan — including what is sent to third-party services during specific scans — because we prefer full transparency over vague statements.

1) What we collect — and what we don't

We do NOT collect or store:

  • Passwords you scan: analyzed entirely in your browser. To check breach databases we use k-anonymity — only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash are sent. The password itself never leaves your device.
  • Messages/URLs analyzed locally: many analyzers run 100% client-side with zero network calls.

What may be sent to an external service for a specific scan (at your request):

Email breach checkThe email address you enterXposedOrNot (public breach database)
Suspicious URL checkThe URL you enterURLhaus (abuse.ch)

AI assistant (when enabled)Your prompt / a condensed scan resultThe model provider via our encrypted proxy

These services process the input solely to return your result. We never sell your data or share it with any party for marketing or advertising.

2) Your account data (if you register)

  • What we store: email, display name (optional), avatar (optional), scan history, academy progress, and settings — hosted on Firebase (Google Cloud) with industry-standard security.
  • Two-factor authentication: when enabled, TOTP secrets are managed by Firebase Auth infrastructure itself and are not stored in our readable databases.
  • Personal API keys: if you add your own key in Settings, it is stored only in your browser (localStorage) and never reaches us.

3) Cookies and local storage

We use browser localStorage for: interface language, theme, assistant chat history, and an anonymous device identifier for abuse prevention. No advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Firebase App Check (reCAPTCHA v3) is used solely for bot protection.

4) Retention and deletion

  • Scan history: kept until you delete it yourself (available from the History page).
  • Your entire account: deletable from Settings → Security → Delete Account, or by request through support — executed within 30 days at most.

5) Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and delete your data, and to withdraw consent at any time (by deleting your account). For privacy-related requests, contact us via the in-app support ticket system or at joetech.dev.systems@gmail.com.

6) Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7) Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the platform evolves; the "last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be announced inside the app.
