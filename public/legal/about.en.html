ABOUT JOESCAN · A JOETECH PRODUCT

Built by JoeTech. Made for people.

JoeScan is a product of JoeTech. We build technology tools and solutions for individuals, and JoeScan is our project in cybersecurity and open-source intelligence (OSINT).

It started with a simple goal: help anyone find out whether their personal information has appeared in a data breach, without needing technical expertise or a paid subscription to get started.

We do not tell you that you are “100% safe” or that all is lost. We give you a realistic picture based on the available data, so you can decide what to do next.

One platform. Different perspectives.

01 / EMAIL

Email exposure checks

Look for your email in known data breaches. When a match is available, reports include the breach name, date, and types of exposed information.

02 / PASSWORDS

Password breach checks

Check whether a password appears in the available database of compromised passwords, without sending the password in plain text.

03 / LINKS

Link analysis

Assess warning signs and known threat signals in a link before opening it. A result is a risk assessment, not a guarantee that a website is safe.

04 / OSINT

Public information checks

Explore information publicly available online about yourself or an authorized subject. Use these tools responsibly and respect other people's privacy.

Reports explain the findings and offer practical next steps, with risk levels and security scores where supported by the tool.

Our principles

We ask only for the information needed for a check. Passwords are checked without being stored or transmitted in plain text. When a check uses an external data source, we explain that in our Privacy & Data policy.

CONTEXT MATTERS

“No match” is not “no risk.”

JoeScan is an initial assessment tool. It does not replace a password manager, two-factor authentication, or a full cybersecurity review.

Our sources do not cover every breach in the world. A result showing no known exposure means “not found in the sources checked”, not “confirmed 100% safe.”
