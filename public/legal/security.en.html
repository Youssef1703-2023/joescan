Security & Responsible Disclosure

We treat security reports as gifts, not threats. Last reviewed: September 1, 2026

How to report a vulnerability

Found something? Please tell us before public disclosure:

  • Email: joetech.dev.systems@gmail.com (PGP available on request)
  • Or encrypted via the in-app support ticket system

Include: affected URL/endpoint, reproduction steps, and impact assessment. Please avoid automated scanning against production and never access other users' data.

Our commitment

  • Acknowledgment within 72 hours, a triage decision within 7 days.
  • No legal action against good-faith research that respects user privacy and stays within the scope below.
  • Public credit (with your consent) after the fix ships — see the hall of thanks below.
  • Active contributors may receive JoeScan Pro time as appreciation (discretionary, at our sole discretion).

Scope

In scope: joescan.me and any subdomain, the Cloudflare Worker API endpoints, and the browser extensions/PWA.

Out of scope: social engineering of staff, physical attacks, denial-of-service volume tests, spam, and anything requiring access to another user's data.

Safe harbor

If you make a good-faith effort to comply with this policy during your research, we will consider your conduct authorized, will not initiate legal action, and will work with you to understand and resolve the issue quickly.
